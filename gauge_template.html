How the score works

Each indicator gives a signal from −1 to +1. The weighted average maps to a score from 0 to 100.

0–20
Strong sell 20–40
Sell 40–60
Neutral 60–80
Buy 80–100
Strong buy

What drives it

Rainbow, Puell and the halving cycle carry 55% of the weight. RSI, VWAP and price-to-200-day are read contrarian; moving-average crosses and MACD follow the trend.
