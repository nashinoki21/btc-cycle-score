How the score works

Each indicator gives a signal from −1 to +1. The weighted average maps to 0–100: under 20 strong sell, 20–40 sell, 40–60 neutral, 60–80 buy, over 80 strong buy.

What drives it

Rainbow, Puell and the halving cycle carry 55% of the weight. RSI, VWAP and price-to-200-day are read contrarian; moving-average crosses and MACD follow the trend.
